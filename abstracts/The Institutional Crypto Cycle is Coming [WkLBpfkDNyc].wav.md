## Abstract

PART 1 — ABSTRACT

This conversation focuses on the delayed but potentially transformative adoption of crypto assets and blockchain-based infrastructure by large, traditional financial institutions, such as pension funds, endowments, insurance companies, and sovereign wealth funds. It discusses why these entities have so far only cautiously engaged with the crypto sector, highlighting both progress and persistent barriers to entry, such as regulatory uncertainty and operational friction. The discussion matters because institutional involvement could significantly legitimize, deepen liquidity for, and accelerate the maturation of the digital asset market, with far-reaching consequences for global finance. Unresolved questions center on what specific catalysts are needed to trigger meaningful institutional flows and how the industry will overcome obstacles related to trust, custodial solutions, and evolving regulation.

PART 2 — KEY IDEAS

- Institutional involvement in crypto so far has been modest; major asset allocators have barely begun to participate.
- Regulatory uncertainty and operational infrastructure gaps pose major barriers to entry for large institutions.
- Some "institutional flows" have begun but represent only a fraction of the eventual potential adoption.
- True institutional engagement is expected to be a multi-year, possibly cyclical process, not a sudden event.
- The maturation and professionalization of crypto market infrastructure are necessary to meet institutional standards.
- Institutions require robust custodial, compliance, and risk management solutions before committing significant capital.
- The integration of crypto with traditional finance could profoundly reshape financial markets and products.
- There are tensions between crypto's open, decentralized ethos and institutional requirements for control and predictability.
- Unresolved risks include regulatory clampdowns, technology failures, and the possibility of misaligned incentives between crypto-native and traditional actors.
- The shift to institutional adoption may blunt some crypto ideals but bring much-needed scale, liquidity, and legitimacy.
